01b · DAYLIGHT MODE — FOR SUNLIT SCREENS In sunlight, a MacBook Air (about 500 nits, glossy) reflects more light than dark pixels give off. Daylight mode uses a near-white ground and near-black ink, has only two text levels, and uses heavier weights (500 body, 700 glance). Accents are darker and more saturated. The photo sits on a mid-grey mat, not white. Toggle ☀ in the top bar or the theme tweak. Everything is driven by CSS custom properties on [data-theme].

05 · LAYOUT AT 1440–1680 Shoot is a 3-column grid: 248px | minmax(0,1fr) | 432px. The side columns stay fixed, so every extra pixel above 1440 goes to the photo. At 1680 the stage is about 1000 px wide. Vertical budget at 900 px: 48 top bar, then 30 honesty band when needed, then the stage (flex), then the 204 brightness inspector, 92 filmstrip and 60 voice bar. Below 820 px of height, the inspector collapses to its headline row. The shot list collapses to a 56 px icon rail with [. The coach panel never collapses, because it holds the glance text.
