Aperture Ally — five marks

Each mark is one neutral plus a single accent, matching the app’s rule that only one thing gets colour. Shown large, as an app icon, at favicon size, and on light.
1a
Aperture Ally
One blade lit A six-blade iris. Five blades are the camera; the lit one is the coach working alongside them. Reads as “aperture” instantly, even at 16px.
1b
aperture ally
The lens that talks A lens barrel with a speech tail — the coach’s voice in your ear at the camera. Friendliest of the five; lowercase wordmark leans into that.

APERTURE ALLY
Two A’s, one focus Interlocked A’s — photographer and coach sharing a stance. Where they cross, the accent dot sits like a focus point. Most monogram-like; pairs with a technical mono wordmark.
